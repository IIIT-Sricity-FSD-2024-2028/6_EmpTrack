Escalate Issue to HR/Admin

When to Escalate to HR/Admin?

  • Team Conflicts: Disputes between team members that you cannot resolve
  • Performance Issues: Persistent underperformance despite warnings
  • Policy Violations: Breach of company policies or code of conduct
  • Harassment/Misconduct: Any form of harassment or unprofessional behavior
  • Resource Needs: Budget, hiring, or resource requests beyond your authority
  • Personal Issues: Your own workplace concerns or requests
