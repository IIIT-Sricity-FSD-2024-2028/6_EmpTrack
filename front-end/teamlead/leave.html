Key protocols for Team Leads

    Minimum 3 days required for planned vacation leave.

  • Team Coverage

    Ensure adequate coverage before approving overlaps.

  • Leave Balance

    Verify balance > requested days strictly.

  • Unauthorized Absence

    No action required; auto-escalates to HR globally.

  • Emergency Leave

    Can be processed retroactively after 48 hrs.

  • Documentation

    Required file upload for sick leave > 2 days.
